Current 60-second storyboard. Supersedes v4's couple-like casting and gestures. Artwork stays text-free, with no UI or AI suggestion panels. Follow four friends/colleagues of different ages, with individual attention, hands-only interaction shots and group experiences. The table and objects remain the main subject.

## Casting and framing

Use ordinary adults of varied ages and backgrounds. Preserve clothing and identities across shots. Place participants on different sides of the table; show varied expressions and attention rather than constant smiles. Avoid romantic gestures, feeding someone, intimate pair poses or a central hero couple. Solo participation and individual preferences should feel equally welcome.

Preserve the supplied reference's warm stone, walnut tray, ceramic, paper and natural light. Match-cut the rail token to train scenery and the noodle bowl to the real food counter. Travel cutaways evoke possible experiences. Object surfaces are illustrated without lettering, and no captions or typography appear in the artwork.
