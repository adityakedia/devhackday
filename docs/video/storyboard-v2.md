# Combo — 60-second advertisement + product demo

**Superseded by `storyboard-v3.md`.** The user clarified that real-time preference interpretation and collaborative tangible-table co-creation are central. This version remains as an earlier creative direction.

**Find your trip. Keep what mattered.**

Lead with the appeal of Taipei, demonstrate the app within five seconds, show a visible consequence of changing the collection, then connect a lived experience to Reflection. Approximately 42 seconds show the product; 18 seconds establish the destination, human payoff and brand.

## Storyboard and script

| Shot | Time | Picture / action | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | 00–05 | Tea pouring, rainy street, someone browsing a book. Quick sensory opening. Headline: “Your Taipei. Your way.” | “Tea shops. Rainy streets. Bookstores. Which Taipei is yours?” |
| 02 | 05–12 | Cut directly into Combo, Planning mode. Select tea, rain, train and bookstore fragments. Each click adds an object to the visible collection. | “With Combo, pick what draws you in.” |
| 03 | 12–20 | Group tea with rain and train with bookstore. Show the resulting “Rainy Literary Taipei” reading, with two alternatives. Hold the readable line: “Bookstores, rainy walks, tea breaks.” | “Bring your choices together. Explore a trip of bookstores, rainy walks and tea breaks.” |
| 04 | 20–28 | Remove tea. Same view, changed collection. The reading becomes “Rainy City Wandering.” Hold the explicit difference: “Fewer tea breaks. More wandering.” | “Want more wandering? Remove the tea. See how your direction changes.” |
| 05 | 28–36 | Click “Save this direction.” Show “Saved” and the chosen title; a quick push toward the paperback prepares the transition. | “Try another combination, or save the one you want to explore.” |
| 06 | 36–43 | Match-cut the book into a real Taipei bookstore. Two friends share a discovery and laugh. Brief overlay: “Go make it yours.” | “Then go make it yours.” |
| 07 | 43–54 | Return to Combo, Reflection mode. Add memory fragments; group rain with umbrella and train with friend. Mark “Friend laughing” central. Select “Ordinary moments” from three possible readings. | “Afterward, collect your memories. Connect the rainy breakfast, the train ride, the friend who made you laugh. Discover what stayed with you.” |
| 08 | 54–60 | Combo end card. Small Planning and Reflection views flank the headline “Find your trip. Keep what mattered.” CTA: “Explore Taipei.” | “Combo. Find your trip. Keep what mattered.” |

## Edit direction

- The opening uses quick destination details, not a long contemplative object close-up.
- Use deliberate, readable clicks and drags. Tea removal and its changed reading are the central demo proof; keep both visible together.
- Keep the same screen framing across shots 03–04 so the difference is immediately apparent.
- Music has a light, warm pulse. Tea pouring, selection clicks, paper movement, rain and laughter punctuate the cuts.
- Feelings come from concrete choices, the person's control and the shared travel moment. Avoid explaining the product's internal AI architecture.
- Add final text and cursor movement in the video edit. Generated typography is only storyboard guidance.
