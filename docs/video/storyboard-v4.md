# People, table, experiences — text-free storyboard

Superseded by `storyboard-v5.md` after the user rejected the couple-focused imagery. The text-free table/experience direction continues with individual attention and a varied small group.

60 seconds, eight shots. Two image sheets contain four shots each, ordered left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Timing and production notes live here, outside the artwork.

| Shot | Time | Image and movement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | 00–06 | Two friends approach the shared table. Empty walnut tray; tactile souvenirs invite curiosity. A shared glance establishes connection. |
| 02 | 06–12 | Close on a fingertip bringing the tea cup into the tray. Pause on contact, ceramic texture and the other person's attentive hand. |
| 03 | 12–20 | Add the illustrated book and rail token. Both people arrange them together, exchanging a smile as the collection takes shape. |
| 04 | 20–27 | Experience cutaway: the same friends share a book on a train, rain at the window and greenery outside. The selected objects evoke a human moment. |
| 05 | 27–35 | Return to the table. One person adds the red lantern; the other gestures toward where it belongs. Show playful negotiation. |
| 06 | 35–43 | Together they bring the noodle bowl into the tray. Two different contributions become a shared arrangement. |
| 07 | 43–52 | Experience cutaway: the same friends laugh over noodles beneath warm lanterns at a Taipei food counter. |
| 08 | 52–60 | Return to the completed tray: tea/book left, lantern/noodles right, rail between. Hold on their faces and shared satisfaction. |

The travel scenes are evocative cutaways, not a chronological jump through an entire trip. Match-cut the illustrated train token into the real train, and the bowl into the real food counter. Return to the same table framing to keep the interaction legible.

Use the supplied reference's warm stone, walnut, ceramic, paper and botanical light. Keep the same two people and clothing throughout. Souvenir illustrations replace printed words: a train illustration on the ticket, a street illustration on the book and an unlettered red lantern. The tangible interaction is conveyed through contact, careful arrangement and human response, without showing technical mechanisms.

Sound direction: quiet ceramic contact, paper movement, train rhythm, rain, food-counter ambience and shared laughter over a warm understated musical pulse.
