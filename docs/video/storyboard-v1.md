## Creative direction

**Shape what could be. Discover what mattered.**

Follow one person's relationship with a Taipei trip: curiosity becomes a direction they can shape; afterward, small memories reveal what stayed with them. The emotional progression is curiosity → recognition → agency → connection → reflection.

Use the reference's tactile world: ivory stone, walnut trays, ceramic, glass, paper, botanical shadows, editorial serif type and forest-green controls. Keep movement deliberate and intimate. Objects and interactions carry the story; the app remains visible long enough for a viewer to understand what the person did and why the response changed.

Working format: 16:9, 60 seconds. Three illustrated sheets, each containing three sequential shots. Replace concept interface layers with actual captures as workflows settle, preserving the narrative, objects and shot timing.

## Motion and sound

- Start close and quiet; reveal the workspace through a slow pullback.
- Use simple cursor movement and object settling to make selection and grouping readable. Hold the changed interpretation in shot 05 for at least three seconds.
- Match-cut across the same ceramic cup in shots 06–07, then across the rail ticket in shots 07–08. These transitions connect possibilities to memories.
- Score: warm, sparse piano or plucked textures, leaving space for ceramic contact, paper slide, light rain and a brief laugh. Avoid a rising technology-ad crescendo.
- Add final typography, captions and cursor animation in the edit; generated lettering is directional artwork.
